# Sprint 1 — LabLoans

## Sprint Goal
By the end of the sprint, the technician can check equipment out to a student and check it back in.

## Selected items
- [F] As a technician, I need to check equipment out to a student so I know who currently has it.
- [F] As a technician, I need to check equipment back in to close the loan.

## Definition of Done
- [ ] Technician can record a checkout with student ID, item name, and date
- [ ] Technician can record a checkin that closes the open loan
- [ ] Technician can view a list of all currently open loans
